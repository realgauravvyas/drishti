How it works

  1. The organiser uploads the album once. Every face in every photo is detected and turned into a numeric fingerprint.
  2. You upload one selfie. It is compared against those fingerprints in memory and then discarded — your selfie is never saved to disk.
  3. You get your photos. Ranked by confidence, with a one-click ZIP of everything you pick.

What this does with your face

Face matching is done entirely on the server that hosts this page — no third-party service, no cloud API, nothing sent anywhere else. The album organiser stores face fingerprints for the photos they uploaded; your selfie is held in memory for the length of one search and never written to disk. Events are deleted automatically after the organiser's retention period. If you would rather not be searchable at all, ask the organiser to remove the photos of you — they can delete individual photos at any time.
